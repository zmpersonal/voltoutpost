Why this stays fresh

Built from updateable public data.

The scheduled GitHub workflow refreshes available PVWatts solar output, EIA residential electricity prices and FEMA disaster-declaration exposure, then rebuilds every city and state page.

01

Solar production

Standardized 10 kW roof-mounted solar simulations provide annual output and month-to-month consistency.

02

Disaster exposure

County-level federal disaster declaration history is used as a simple, transparent exposure proxy—not as an outage prediction.

03

Electricity prices

Residential retail electricity prices provide the cost component and refresh when newer data are available.
